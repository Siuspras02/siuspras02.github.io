3. Turn Flow, Calls & Dora

RON > PON/KAN > CHI
  • Turn: Draw 1 from wall → Declare Tsumo / Closed Kan / Riichi → Discard 1 tile.
  • Chi: Call sequence from left player only (opens hand).
  • Pon: Call triplet from any player (opens hand).
  • Kan: Open (call on discard or upgrade open Pon) or Closed (4 in hand). Draws 1 dead-wall tile + flips 1 Kan Dora.
  • Riichi: Closed hand in Tenpai (1 away) → bet 1,000 stick, discard sideways. Hand locked (only closed Kan allowed if waits unchanged).
  • Win: Tsumo (self-draw; all 3 pay) or Ron (on discard; discarder pays).
  • Dead Wall (14 tiles): 4 replacement tiles + up to 5 Dora & 5 Ura-Dora indicators.

Win Method: Closed Ron +10 · Tsumo +2 (0 if Closed Pinfu)  |  Pair: Dragon/Seat/Round Wind +2 (Double Wind +4)

7. Dora Rotation, Winds & Dragons

INDICATOR → NEXT TILE

Dora Rule: The actual Dora is the next tile in sequence after the face-up indicator on the dead wall (+1 Han per matching tile in hand; Dora ≠ Yaku).

Ura-Dora & Kan-Dora: Flip a new Kan-Dora indicator for every Kan declared. If you win after declaring Riichi, reveal the tiles underneath all active indicators as bonus Ura-Dora.

8. Furiten, Rotation & Counters

  • Furiten (No Ron allowed; Tsumo only): 1. Permanent (any winning tile is in your discards); 2. Temporary (passed on a Ron; clears on your next discard); 3. Riichi Furiten (passed on a winning tile after Riichi → permanent for rest of hand).
  • Dealer Rotation (Renchan): Dealer keeps East if they win or are Tenpai on exhaustive draw; otherwise rotates counter-clockwise (E→S→W→N).
  • Honba (100pt repeat stick): Placed by dealer on dealer win or draw. Adds +300 pts per counter to Ron (+100/payer on Tsumo). Resets when non-dealer wins.
  • Riichi Sticks: 1,000 pts each; winner takes all on table (leftover at game end goes to 1st place).
  • Game Length: Hanchan = East + South rounds (8 hands min). Ends early if any player drops below 0 pts (Tobi).

9. Exhaustive & Abortive Draws

  • Exhaustive Draw (Ryuukyoku): Wall empties with no winner. Players reveal hands: 1 tenpai: +3000 (each noten pays 1000) · 2 tenpai: +1500 each · 3 tenpai: +1000 each (noten pays 3000).
  • Nagashi Mangan: At exhaustive draw, if all your discards are Terminals/Honors and none were called, score as a mangan tsumo: non-dealer 2000/4000, dealer 4000 all.
  • Abortive Draws (Dealer repeats, +1 Honba): Kyuushu Kyuuhai (9+ diff terminals/honors on 1st draw); Suufon Renda (same 4 Winds discarded in 1st turn); Suucha Riichi (all 4 players declare Riichi); Suukaikan (4 Kans declared by 2+ players); Sanchahou (Triple Ron on one discard).
